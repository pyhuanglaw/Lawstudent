一、宿舍簡介 Dormitory Overview

大一女舍位於臺大羅斯福路四段，鄰近捷運站及公館商圈，生活機能便利。宿舍由相鄰的A、B兩棟組成，二樓天橋連通，設有24小時傳達室，提供安全舒適的住宿環境與完善的生活設施。

Freshman Women's Dorm is located on Section 4 of Roosevelt Road, near the MRT station and Gongguan commercial district, offering convenient access to daily amenities. The dorm consists of two adjacent buildings, A and B, connected by a second-floor skybridge, with a 24-hour reception desk, providing a safe and comfortable living environment with comprehensive facilities.

四、一樓傳達室服務 Reception Services

大一女舍傳達室設於A棟一樓，A、B棟共用，24小時提供以下服務:
The reception desk of Freshman Women's Dorm is located on the first floor of Building A, shared by both buildings, and provides the following 24-hour services:

信件與包裹收發（僅收住宿生本人，不收冷藏、冷凍或貨到付款包裹） Mail and parcel collection for residents only (no C.O.D. or perishable items).

五、鄰近生活機能 Neighborhood & Accessibility

大一女舍鄰近公館商圈與校總區主要教學大樓，交通便利、生活機能完善。
Located near the Gongguan commercial district and NTU’s main teaching buildings, the dorm offers convenient access to transportation and daily amenities.
