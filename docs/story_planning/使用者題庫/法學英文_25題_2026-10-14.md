Legal English Quiz — 15 Questions

律師、司法官第一試法學英文模擬題庫

Easy — Questions 1–10

Question 1

The person who brings a civil lawsuit against another person is called the ______.

(A) defendant
(B) plaintiff
(C) witness
(D) judge

Answer: B. plaintiff

解析：Plaintiff 是原告，指提起民事訴訟的人。Defendant 是被告，witness 是證人。

Question 2

Which of the following means “a legally enforceable agreement”?

(A) Contract
(B) Crime
(C) Evidence
(D) Judgment

Answer: A. Contract

解析：Contract 是契約，通常指具有法律拘束力的合意。Judgment 是判決，evidence 是證據。

Question 3

A person who violates criminal law may be punished for committing a ______.

(A) contract
(B) right
(C) crime
(D) duty

Answer: C. crime

解析：Crime 是犯罪。Commit a crime 是常見搭配，意思是實施犯罪行為。

Question 4

Which of the following is responsible for deciding cases in a court?

(A) Witness
(B) Plaintiff
(C) Lawyer
(D) Judge

Answer: D. Judge

解析：Judge 是法官，負責審理案件並作出裁判。Lawyer 是律師。

Question 5

The defendant has the right to hire a ______ to represent him in court.

(A) lawyer
(B) witness
(C) victim
(D) police officer

Answer: A. lawyer

解析：Lawyer 是律師。Represent someone in court 指在法庭上代理或辯護。

Question 6

The police arrested the suspect because they believed he had committed a ______.

(A) judgment
(B) crime
(C) contract
(D) lawsuit

Answer: B. crime

解析：Suspect 是嫌疑人；arrest 是逮捕；commit a crime 是犯罪。

Question 7

Which word means information used to prove facts in court?

(A) Evidence
(B) Punishment
(C) Property
(D) Contract

Answer: A. Evidence

解析：Evidence 是證據，可用來證明案件事實。Property 是財產，punishment 是處罰。

Question 8

If a person disagrees with a court’s judgment, the person may have the right to ______.

(A) arrest
(B) appeal
(C) investigate
(D) testify

Answer: B. appeal

解析：Appeal 是上訴，指依法請求上級法院審查裁判。Testify 是作證。

Question 9

A person who sees an accident and gives testimony in court is a ______.

(A) prosecutor
(B) defendant
(C) witness
(D) legislator

Answer: C. witness

解析：Witness 是證人；testimony 是證詞；give testimony 是作證。

Question 10

The constitution is generally considered the ______ law of a country.

(A) lowest
(B) highest
(C) oldest
(D) shortest

Answer: B. highest

解析：Constitution 是憲法，通常具有國家最高法律位階。Highest law 是最高位階的法律。

Medium — Questions 11–15

Question 11

When one party fails to fulfill its obligations under a contract, this may constitute a ______ of contract.

(A) breach
(B) benefit
(C) defense
(D) remedy

Answer: A. breach

解析：Breach of contract 是違約。Fulfill an obligation 是履行義務；remedy 是法律救濟。

Question 12

In a criminal trial, the prosecution must generally prove the defendant’s guilt beyond a reasonable ______.

(A) right
(B) duty
(C) doubt
(D) claim

Answer: C. doubt

解析：Beyond a reasonable doubt 是「排除合理懷疑」，為刑事定罪的重要證明標準。Prosecution 是檢方，guilt 是有罪。

Question 13

The court ordered the defendant to pay monetary ______ to compensate the plaintiff for the injury.

(A) witnesses
(B) damages
(C) statutes
(D) appeals

Answer: B. damages

解析：Damages 在法律英文中是損害賠償金。Compensate 是賠償、補償；injury 是損害或傷害。

Question 14

A law passed by the legislature is commonly called a ______.

(A) testimony
(B) verdict
(C) statute
(D) contract

Answer: C. statute

解析：Statute 是制定法、成文法律。Legislature 是立法機關；verdict 是裁決，常指陪審團裁決。

Question 15

A person is presumed ______ until proven guilty in a criminal trial.

(A) liable
(B) innocent
(C) negligent
(D) responsible

Answer: B. innocent

解析：Presumed innocent 是推定無罪。Presumption of innocence（無罪推定）是刑事訴訟的重要基本原則。Negligent 是有過失的。

Question 16 — Contract Law

If one party fails to perform a contractual obligation without a legal excuse, the other party may seek a legal ______.

(A) remedy
(B) witness
(C) offense
(D) testimony

Answer: A. remedy

解析：Remedy 是法律救濟。當一方違反契約義務時，他方可能請求損害賠償或其他救濟。Contractual obligation 是契約義務。

Question 17 — Criminal Law

A person who intentionally helps another person commit a crime may be held ______ for the offense.

(A) innocent
(B) liable
(C) legal
(D) valid

Answer: B. liable

解析：Be held liable for 是「因……負法律責任」。Intentionally 是故意地；offense 是犯罪或違法行為。

Question 18 — Constitutional Law

The government must respect the fundamental rights ______ by the constitution.

(A) violated
(B) prohibited
(C) guaranteed
(D) punished

Answer: C. guaranteed

解析：Rights guaranteed by the constitution 是「憲法保障的權利」。Fundamental rights 是基本權利。

Question 19 — Civil Procedure

The plaintiff must provide sufficient evidence to ______ the allegations made in the complaint.

(A) ignore
(B) support
(C) punish
(D) withdraw

Answer: B. support

解析：Support the allegations 是以證據支持主張。Allegation 是當事人的事實主張；complaint 在美國民事訴訟中通常指起訴狀。

Question 20 — Tort Law

A driver who fails to exercise reasonable care and causes injury to another person may be liable for ______.

(A) negligence
(B) inheritance
(C) legislation
(D) jurisdiction

Answer: A. negligence

解析：Negligence 是過失。Reasonable care 是合理注意義務；在符合其他侵權要件時，違反注意義務而造成損害可能成立過失侵權。

Question 21 — Criminal Procedure

The accused has the right to remain silent and is not required to ______ against himself.

(A) appeal
(B) testify
(C) investigate
(D) prosecute

Answer: B. testify

解析：Testify against oneself 是作出不利於自己的證言，涉及不自證己罪原則。The accused 是被告或被指控犯罪的人。

Question 22 — Property Law

The owner of a property generally has the right to possess, use, and ______ it, subject to legal restrictions.

(A) dispose of
(B) accuse of
(C) testify against
(D) appeal from

Answer: A. dispose of

解析：Dispose of property 是處分財產。Possess 是占有；法律上所有權的權能通常包括使用、收益及處分，但可能受到法律限制。

Question 23 — Administrative Law

A government agency must act within the authority ______ to it by law.

(A) denied
(B) granted
(C) violated
(D) appealed

Answer: B. granted

解析：Authority granted by law 是法律授予的權限。Grant 是授予；government agency 是行政機關。

Question 24 — Evidence Law

The court may exclude evidence if it was obtained in violation of the defendant’s constitutional ______.

(A) contracts
(B) properties
(C) rights
(D) obligations

Answer: C. rights

解析：Constitutional rights 是憲法權利。Exclude evidence 是排除證據；違法取得的證據是否排除，須依具體法域及適用規則判斷。

Question 25 — Corporate Law

Directors are generally required to act in good faith and in the best ______ of the company.

(A) interests
(B) punishments
(C) judgments
(D) violations

Answer: A. interests

解析：In the best interests of the company 是「符合公司最佳利益」。Good faith 是善意、誠信；這是公司董事義務常見的法律英文表達。
